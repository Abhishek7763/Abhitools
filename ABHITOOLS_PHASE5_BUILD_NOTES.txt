ABHITOOLS PHASE 5 - DOCUMENTS & BORROWER PHOTO

Added:
- Borrower photo upload from Advanced Borrower Profile.
- Photos stored in Supabase public photos bucket and borrower photo_url updated server-side.
- Private document upload for Aadhaar, PAN, Loan Agreement, Receipt and Other.
- Optional document-to-loan linking.
- Private document viewing through short-lived signed URLs (15 minutes).
- Secure document deletion removes both storage object and metadata.
- 8 MB server-side upload limit.
- JPG, PNG, WEBP photos; PDF/JPG/PNG/WEBP documents.
- Borrower and loan ownership validation before upload.
- Upload/photo/delete activity logging.
- Mobile and dark-mode document/photo UI.

Security:
- All upload/document APIs require the signed admin session.
- Private documents are never exposed through public permanent URLs.
- SUPABASE_SERVICE_KEY stays server-side only.
- Supabase Security Advisor checked after Phase 5 migration: 0 issues.

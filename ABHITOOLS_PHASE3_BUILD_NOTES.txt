ABHITOOLS PHASE 3 - AUTOMATIC DUE & OVERDUE ENGINE

Features:
- Server-side automatic EMI status refresh using Asia/Kolkata business date.
- Paid stays paid; unpaid/partial past-due EMI becomes overdue automatically.
- Legacy EMI rows with no year/due_date are never guessed or auto-overdue.
- New /api/due endpoint with sanitized public summary and admin IDs for payment actions.
- Admin dashboard: overdue amount/count, today, tomorrow, next 7 days, this month.
- Admin Due Center with Overdue/Today/Tomorrow/Next 7/Month lists and Pay shortcut.
- Public dashboard: overdue, tomorrow and next-7-day summaries.
- Payment add/edit/undo immediately recalculates overdue state correctly.

No new environment variables are required.

Scope

- Apply is atomic through public.abhi_apply_legacy_due_dates(...).
- Automatic full DB snapshot is created in the same transaction before data changes.
- Due statuses are refreshed after successful date cleanup.
- Successful cleanup writes LEGACY_DATE_CLEANUP to activity_log.
- RPC execution is revoked from public/anon/authenticated and granted only to service_role.

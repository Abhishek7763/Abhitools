AbhiTools Phase 20 — Collection Priority & Account Health Insights
Date: 2026-08-24

Purpose
-------
Adds an admin-only operational collection insights center without creating a credit score or automated lending decision system.

What is included
----------------
- New Collection Insights center in Admin Controls and Phase 19 Command Center quick actions.
- New consolidated route: /api/dashboard?mode=risk (still uses existing dashboard Vercel function).
- Borrower-level operational tiers: Critical, High, Watch, Current, Data Incomplete.
- Collection Priority Score (0–100) only when enough verified dated EMI data exists.
- Data-quality guard: if less than 50% of current EMI records have verified due dates, score is suppressed and borrower is shown as Data Incomplete.
- Signals: current overdue share, maximum days overdue, partial EMI count, observed late-paid dated EMI history, 30-day reminder-contact gap and contact availability.
- Portfolio concentration: top borrower exposure and top-3 exposure share.
- Dated EMI coverage, observed late-payment rate and missing-contact metrics.
- Search, tier filtering and operational sorting.
- Quick actions: Borrower Profile, Reminder Center, WhatsApp, Call and Data Quality review.
- CSV export.
- Mobile and dark-mode styling.

Safety / interpretation
-----------------------
- Not a credit score.
- Not intended for automated loan approval, rejection, pricing or eligibility.
- Missing legacy dates are never treated as delinquency.
- No borrower/loan/payment data is changed by this phase.

Infrastructure

- No new Vercel serverless function.
